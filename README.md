## 📖 About the Project

**Jharkhand Trails** is a tourism discovery platform focused on showcasing the diverse destinations of Jharkhand, India.

Jharkhand is home to spectacular waterfalls, dense forests, scenic hills, wildlife, lakes, dams, temples, historical places, tribal heritage, and many lesser-known destinations.

The purpose of Jharkhand Trails is to bring these destinations together in one clean, simple, and engaging platform where travelers can discover places worth visiting.

The platform is designed with a focus on:

- 🌿 Nature and responsible tourism
- 💧 Waterfalls and natural attractions
- ⛰️ Hill stations and scenic locations
- 🐘 Wildlife destinations
- 🏞️ Lakes and dams
- 🛕 Cultural and tribal heritage
- 📍 District-wise exploration
- 🔎 Easy destination discovery
- 👤 Personalized user experiences

# ✨ Features

## 🗺️ Explore Destinations

Discover different tourist destinations throughout Jharkhand.

Destinations can be explored through categories such as:

- 💧 Waterfalls
- ⛰️ Hill Stations
- 🐘 Wildlife
- 🌿 Nature
- 🛕 Tribal Heritage
- 🏞️ Dams & Lakes
- 🌄 Viewpoints
- 🏛️ Historical Places

---

## 🔎 Search Destinations

Search for destinations and districts to quickly find places to explore.

The platform is designed to make destination discovery simple and intuitive.

---

## 📍 Destination Details

Each destination can have its own dedicated page containing relevant information about the location.

Destination information can include:

- Destination name
- District
- Description
- Category
- Tags
- Images
- Visit information
- Related destinations

---

## 🏞️ Browse by District

Explore destinations based on districts and regions of Jharkhand.

This makes it easier for travelers to discover multiple attractions located around the same area.

---

## 👤 User Authentication

Users can create an account and securely access personalized functionality.

Authentication includes:

- Sign Up
- Login
- Logout
- Session management
- Protected user functionality

Authentication is implemented using **Passport.js** and Express sessions.

---

## ✅ Track Visited Places

Users can keep track of destinations they have already visited.

This allows travelers to create their own personal travel history within the platform.

---
